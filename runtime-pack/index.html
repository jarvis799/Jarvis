After updating

Settings → Voice Unlock: enable, add/edit/delete phrases, record/play private samples, test recognition and manage permissions.

Settings → Liquid Glass Lock Screen: enable the companion screen, select a grayscale Gallery wallpaper and preview the clock, voice animation, camera and flashlight. Android may require tapping its notification.

Settings → Offline AI Models: a ready local model no longer needs a Gemini key when no online provider is configured. Enable “Use internet with local AI” for optional web search.

A phrase or saved sample is not proof of identity. Android PIN, pattern, password and biometrics remain in control. Gemma E2B needs about 3 GB of storage plus sufficient free RAM; actual on-device inference testing determines readiness.
